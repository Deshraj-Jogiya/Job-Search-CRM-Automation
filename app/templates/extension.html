Browser extension{% if version %} v{{ version }}{% endif %}

On an employer's application page, the extension finds the form's fields (including ones inside iframes and shadow DOM), asks this instance for answers grounded in your profile, fills them, and attaches the tailored resume and cover letter as PDFs. It runs in your own browser on your own connection, which is how it gets past the bot blocking that stops server-side autofill on some employer sites. It never submits: you review every field and press Submit yourself.

Left for you on purpose: open-ended questions ("why do you want to work here"), consent and legal acknowledgments, hidden honeypot fields meant for bots, and any choice whose options don't clearly match a fact on record.

Install (one time)

In Chrome open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select the repository's extension/ folder. Click its toolbar icon, enter this instance's address and Save, and stay signed in here in the same browser: the extension uses that session, so there is no separate sign-in or key. After that, opening an Approved application's page fills it on its own. Full steps and the reason for its permissions are in extension/README.md.
